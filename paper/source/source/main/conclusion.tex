\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
After an environment-model change, using an encoded negative to exclude a
continuation requires both preservation and query/objective correspondence.
For the one-way helper encoding, a safe response now does not ensure
preservation: one source-winning strategy must maintain the first-change
response option. With retained duties, that response must enter a region
that remains safe. Patrol demonstrates the distinction through a positive
policy and an argument covering all safe strategies. Vehicle and Obstacle
connect preservation to initialization and tool queries, including the
controlled false-to-true result at exact B. The characterization and executable
case checks make candidate-exclusion reasoning in replacement planning
explicit and inspectable.
