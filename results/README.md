# Results: start from a claim

The rows below lead to public guides and their exact archives. They are reading routes, not additional experimental populations. The [complete machine-readable index](../EVIDENCE_READER_INDEX.json) and [claim-to-file map](../CLAIM_TO_FILE_INDEX.json) retain individual members and historical scopes.

| Question in the paper | Start with | What the evidence establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why can positive initialization miss continuation loss? (IV-A, Vehicle) | [Whole-region Vehicle188 evidence](../VEHICLE188_WHOLE_REGION_EVIDENCE.md) · [completed native queries](../VEHICLE188_NATIVE_EVIDENCE.md) | 1,408 lost source states among 8,064 source winners; both initial judgments remain positive. Reachability is existential per target, on one outcome-selected input. |
| Why is a local safe response insufficient? (IV-B, Patrol) | [Continuation evidence](../CONTINUATION_EVIDENCE.md) | Source-controller reuse, retained-entry loss, countercertificate, saved reduced-query and manager evidence. Full-helper native queries remain UNKNOWN. |
| Why does a solved game not certify its extracted graph? (V, Lift) | [Continuation evidence](../CONTINUATION_EVIDENCE.md) · [matched Lift selection study](../LIFT_CAUSAL_EXTENSION.md) | A fixed strict-only graph and a diagnostic selection contrast; this is not a general repair method or a historical/default-frontend claim. |
| What are the paused-update consequences? (VI-C) | [Pending-work handoff](../PENDING_NATIVE_HANDOFF.md) · [diagnostic evidence](../PENDING_DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE.md) | Fixed pending-work boundaries and bounded executions; no deployment or performance advantage is inferred. |
| What do the existing-method comparisons show? (VI-D) | [Normalized eleven](../NORMALIZED_ELEVEN.md) · [historical C14/R11/C59 index](../EVIDENCE_READER_INDEX.txt) | Fixed rosters, distinct denominators, admitted graphs, refusals, failures, and UNKNOWNs remain separate. |
| What happened on the seven known positive/control inputs? (VI-D, Supplement Y) | [Fixed-seven native evidence](../FIXED_SEVEN_NATIVE_EVIDENCE.md) | Fourteen completed native queries and 198,672 saved-state comparisons; three related active variants and four identity controls. No prevalence or general preservation claim. |
| Where are negative and unresolved outcomes? | [Bounded Intruder attempt](../INTRUDER_BOUNDED_EVIDENCE.md) · [residual census](../RESIDUAL_CENSUS.md) · [complete index](../EVIDENCE_READER_INDEX.txt) | Timeouts, partial data, UNKNOWNs, dormant interventions, and source-binding limits are retained. |
| Where are additional certificates and finite studies? | [Certificate and finite-study guide](../CERTIFICATE_FINITE_EVIDENCE.md) · [noninitial handoff](../NONINITIAL_HANDOFF.md) | Separately qualified certificates and constructed finite cases, with their own selection and execution boundaries. |

For each route, read the guide's **results**, **replay**, and **limits** together. Native-process records, saved BDD membership checks, source proofs, graph audits, and bounded executions support different conclusions. [reproduce/](../reproduce/README.md) explains which operations can be repeated from the public files.
